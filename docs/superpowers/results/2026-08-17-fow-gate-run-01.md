## How to read this

The FoW verdict is the **visual observation**, recorded by hand — not something the
mission script decides. `allied_support_cmd_gate_auto$` records only whether the hold
was released explicitly or by the 60s backstop.

GoH offers no console for setting a mission variable mid-game, so on a supervised run
the backstop is the expected release path and `gate_auto = 1` is normal. It does not
invalidate the observation. What invalidates a result is nobody watching during the
hold — hence the "Observed for the full hold?" field.

A **FAIL** at stage 3 means the cloned actor does not illuminate terrain while
human-owned. That retires candidate A as the production birth path, and the commander
is irrelevant until a birth mechanism passes this gate.
